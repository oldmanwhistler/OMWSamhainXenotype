# AI Disclosure

This is a free mod with an AI disclosure so you can easily avoid it if that is your prerogative.

## How AI was used

- [Atlas](https://github.com/lordfelixmotosr/atlas)
  - scaffolding, publishing, reviewing, testing, machine translation
- GTP-6 Luna
  - 5 gene icons
  - 1 xenotype icon

## Anti-AI

Seeing an AI disclosure and leaving a rude comment won't get rid of the AI but will get rid of people and companies using AI disclosures.

There's so much negative stuff around AI that it is very understandable to lash out at it, but please try to direct it into useful avenues like how you vote.

[These are my tips for how I handle the deluge of AI generated mods](https://github.com/oldmanwhistler/OMWRimworldModIdeas/blob/main/Slop.md).

Whether or not someone used AI does not tell you how much effort was put into this. For instance, in this little XML only mod I still had a two hour debug to find an unlogged crash to desktop when I was testing it in with 300 mods.

The bug is in a biotech patch with 11k subscribers and I was able to change how I wrote my genes to work around it.

Modding with AI still has all the hair-pulling moments that modding without AI does.
